A space for community, curiosity, and hand-crafted creations

Cup of Intuition explores spirituality, deconstruction, social justice, and the everyday art of navigating life. Subscribers get full access to new essays and the publication archives—perfect for slow mornings or late-night reflections.

But this space is more than just a blog. Cup of Intuition is a home for community, creativity, and hand-crafted creations—from thoughtful essays to artisanal products like soaps and oils. Pull up a chair and explore, comment, or support the work that keeps these conversations brewing. Whether you’re deconstructing faith, exploring justice, or simply curious, you’re welcome here.

Meet the creator: Witchy writer, social worker, friend, and community advocate. Just sipping on a cup of intuition as I navigate this thing called life.
